# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 4, 2026**

This policy describes how Pump Dump ("we," "us," "the app") collects, uses, and
protects your information. Pump Dump is a social app for weightlifters to log
workouts, track personal records, and share progress with people they follow.

## Who we are

Pump Dump is operated by **Pump Dump**. Questions about this
policy or your data can be sent to **massspurs5@gmail.com**.

## Information we collect

**Account information.** Email address and password (handled by our
authentication provider, Supabase Auth — we never see or store your raw
password). If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the email address
and name associated with that account, and a unique identifier from that
provider.

**Profile information you choose to add.** Display name, bio, avatar photo,
gym name, favorite lift, and years lifting. All of these are optional.

**Content you post.** Workout posts, including photos, captions, muscle-group
tags, and logged lifts (exercise, weight, reps, and unit). Comments and likes
you leave on posts. Posts you save (bookmark).

**Social graph.** Who you follow and who follows you, and — if your account
is private — pending follow requests. Accounts you block or mute (this is
private to you; the other person is not notified).

**Safety reports.** If you report a post or account, we store the report and
the reason you gave, so it can be reviewed.

**What we do not collect.** We do not collect your location. We do not use
advertising SDKs or third-party analytics/tracking libraries. We do not sell
your data to anyone, for any reason.

## How we use your information

- To operate the core features of the app: your feed, posts, profile,
  follows, comments, likes, notifications, and search.
- To calculate personal records and posting streaks from your logged
  workouts.
- To enforce safety features you or others use: blocking, muting, and
  reviewing reported content.
- To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not build advertising
profiles about you.

## Who we share it with

- **Supabase** — our database, authentication, and file-storage provider.
  Your account data, posts, and photos are stored on Supabase's
  infrastructure under their security and privacy commitments.
- **Apple and Google** — only if you choose to sign in using "Sign in with
  Apple" or Google Sign-In, in which case those providers handle the
  authentication handshake per their own privacy policies.
- **Other users, as intended by the app's design** — your display name, bio,
  avatar, posts, and lift history are visible to your followers (or to
  everyone, if your account is public). Your followers/following lists are
  visible per the same rule. If you set your account to private, only
  approved followers can see your posts.

We do not share your data with data brokers, advertisers, or any other third
party beyond what's described above.

## Your choices and rights

- **Edit or remove content** at any time from within the app (edit your
  profile, delete individual posts).
- **Control who sees your content** by making your account private, and
  control who can follow you via follow-request approval.
- **Block or mute** any account, at any time, from that account's profile.
- **Delete your account** at any time from Settings → Delete Account. This
  permanently removes your profile, posts, photos, comments, likes, follows,
  and reports you filed. This action cannot be undone.
- **Request a copy of your data or ask questions** by emailing
  **massspurs5@gmail.com**.

## Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you
delete your account, your data is permanently removed as described above,
except where we're legally required to retain certain records for a limited
period (for example, to investigate abuse reports filed against you before
deletion).

## Children's privacy

Pump Dump is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has
created an account, contact us at **massspurs5@gmail.com** and we will remove it.

## Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data, including
encrypted connections (HTTPS) and database-level access rules that restrict
who can read or write which data. No method of storage or transmission is
100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "Last updated"
date above and, where required, notify you in the app.

## Contact

Delete your account

Permanent, in-app, no waiting period.

You can delete your Pump Dump account directly from the app, at any time:

  • Open Pump Dump and sign in
  • Go to Profile → Settings
  • Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  • Confirm twice — this step is deliberately hard to trigger by accident

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, posts, photos, comments, likes, follows, saved posts, notifications, blocks/mutes, and any reports you filed. This action is immediate and cannot be undone — there is no recovery period and no way for us to restore a deleted account.

Don't have the app installed?

Email massspurs5@gmail.com from the email address on your account and request deletion. We'll verify it's you and delete your account and data the same way the in-app flow does.

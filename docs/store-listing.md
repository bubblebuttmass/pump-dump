## Full description

Pump Dump is the social app for people who actually lift.

Log your workouts, track every set, and watch your personal records climb —
then share the pump with lifters who get it. No generic step-counting, no
influencer noise. Just training, tracked honestly and shared with people who
follow you because they lift too.

**Log it your way**
Track sets, reps, and weight for any exercise, in kg or lb. Add a caption
and photos, tag the muscle group you hit, and post — as much or as little
detail as you want.

**Personal records, automatically**
Pump Dump calculates your estimated 1-rep max after every set and tells you
the moment you hit a new PR. No spreadsheets, no manual math.

**A feed that's actually about training**
Follow other lifters and see their workouts, PRs, and streaks — not ads,
not algorithm bait. Like, comment, and save the posts that motivate you.

**Built for privacy from day one**
Go private and approve your own followers. Block or mute anyone, anytime,
silently. Delete any post — or your whole account — whenever you want, no
questions asked.

**Streaks and badges that mean something**
Build a real training streak. Unlock achievements tied to actual
milestones — workouts logged, PRs hit, consistency kept — not hollow
gamification.

Whether you're chasing a new squat max or just trying to show up
consistently, Pump Dump is where you log the work and let people see it.

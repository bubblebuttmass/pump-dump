Accessibility

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Pump Dump supports the following device accessibility settings. These follow your device's system settings automatically — there's nothing to turn on inside the app itself.

Reduced Motion

If you have Reduce Motion turned on in your device settings, Pump Dump detects it and skips or shortens its animations and transitions.

Sufficient Contrast

Text and interactive elements are built to meet standard contrast guidelines against the app's background, so content stays readable at a glance.

Larger Text

Pump Dump respects your device's text-size setting. Increase the text size in your device's display settings and text throughout the app scales with it.

Dark Interface

Pump Dump uses a dark interface throughout, by design — there is currently no light mode.

What's not yet supported

Pump Dump has not been fully tested with screen readers (VoiceOver, TalkBack). Some elements may not be labeled correctly yet. This is an area we're actively improving.

Feedback

If you hit an accessibility barrier using Pump Dump, or have a request, email massspurs5@gmail.com — we read every message and prioritize fixes.
